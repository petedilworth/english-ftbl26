How the process works

  1. The record begins on {{ epoch_label }}, the first day of the {{ first_season_label }} season. Count the days from then to the day you were born.
  2. Take the league table as it stood at the end of that day: every club in the top four divisions, top to bottom. There were ninety-two of them in every season but one.
  3. Divide the day count by the number of clubs. The remainder, counted down from the top, is a place in that table. The club in it is yours.

So consecutive birthdays walk down the table one place at a time, everyone born on the same day gets the same club, and over any three months every place is handed out. It is arbitrary and it is fair. The table for a day is worked out from the {{ match_count }} dated results in the record, ranked under that season's own rules{% if rule_notes %}: {{ rule_notes }}{% endif %}. Points deducted during a season are applied when it ends. Someone born before the record begins gets the same day of the year in its first season. The notice shows its working, and the rule is in the open.
