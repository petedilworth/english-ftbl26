    Each dot is a club at the deprivation of the people it draws (official weights), by the tier it plays in this season. The dashed line is England.

    The orange tick is the tier's median.     How it is worked out

    Source: the English Indices of Deprivation 2025 (MHCLG, Open Government Licence), File 7, for every small area in England, joined to neighbourhoods with the ONS 2021 lookup and averaged by population. Each measure is turned into a percentile of England's people, so they can be weighted against each other; at the official weights the result tracks the published index closely but is not it – MHCLG combines transformed ranks that lean harder on the very worst places. A club's figure is the average percentile of the people it draws, weighted by the catchment model's shares, the same model as the income page. The indices rank places against each other: they say where is more deprived, not by how much, and they cover England only, so Welsh clubs are not here. Fetched {{ fetched }}.
