Three measures, three orders. Longest bounce run is the purest yo-yo: moves in consecutive seasons, each the reverse of the last. Moves per season is promotions plus relegations over finished seasons. Restlessness multiplies that by the number of tiers the club has spanned, so a club that fell from the top flight to the fifth tier and climbed back scores beside one that bounces between two divisions. The {{ shown }} traces here are the top {{ top }} on any of the three.
