How it is worked out

Odds: the average price across bookmakers in the football-data.co.uk files – the site's own average in recent seasons, Betbrain's before that, and the mean of the bookmakers listed in the earliest seasons – with the margin taken out in proportion. Expected points for each side are three times its chance of winning plus its chance of a draw. The market reprices every week, so a club that improves mid-season stops looking lucky within a month; the odds understate luck that lasts. Shots: each side's shots on target, times the share of shots on target that became goals in that division that season, as the average of a Poisson score. That is not expected goals – there is no shot location in the data – and it judges a match by what happened in it. A season is ranked only when at least {{ (min_coverage * 100) | int }}% of its matches have the data; where some are missing, those matches count at the points actually won. Shots data starts in {{ season(first_s) if first_s else "–" }} and is thinner in tier 5.
