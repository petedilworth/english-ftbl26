How it is worked out

From the accounts each club's company files at Companies House: the tagged electronic accounts read directly, the paper ones from scans, and for the larger clubs results reported when the accounts came out, each row linked to its source. A group's consolidated accounts and the club company's own are kept apart, marked group where the figures are the group's; two years are compared only when they are the same company at the same level. Wages are staff costs as filed, which for most clubs include the players and exclude transfer fees. A balance sheet whose parts do not add up is left out whole{% if flagged.does_not_balance %} ({{ flagged.does_not_balance }} club{{ "s" if flagged.does_not_balance != 1 }}){% endif %}, because which figure is wrong cannot be told. A figure the reader had to correct carries a †, with the reason on hover: {% for k, v in doubt_labels.items() %}{{ v }}{% if not loop.last %}; {% endif %}{% endfor %}.
