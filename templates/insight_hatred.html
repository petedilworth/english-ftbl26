How it is scored

Envied: a title is three points and a top-four finish one, since 1958/59; the last ten seasons count double. Resented: wage bill rank within the division in the latest accounts, up to four points; financial-rules deductions in the top flight, two each; and ownership events from the curated list. Mocked: since the last title, a tenth of a point per top-flight season and a point per top-four finish. Despised: the curated list only, weighted one to five. Exposure — clubs within fifteen miles and people in reach — is shown in the detail and never multiplied in.

The index: each kind divided by the highest score any club has in it, so the leader of each scores 1 and the rest a fraction of that. The four fractions are then averaged with the slider weights and multiplied by 100. The scaling is what makes equal weights mean equal: without it envy, whose raw scores run to 86, would outvote despised, which stops at 5. The catch is that a kind with one runaway leader – mocked, where Tottenham score twice the next club – leaves everyone else in that kind small.

Every weight is in src/hatred.py and every curated entry in content/hatred.yml, with a note saying what it refers to.
