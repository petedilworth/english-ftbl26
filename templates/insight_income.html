Does money around the ground buy tiers?

Each dot is a club at the average income of the people it draws, by the tier it plays in this season. The line is England.

The orange tick is the tier's median. Across {{ model.n }} clubs the correlation between catchment income and tier is {{ model.corr_income }}; between catchment size and tier it is {{ model.corr_people }}. Size alone explains {{ (model.r2_people * 100) | round | int }}% of the variance in tier; adding income takes that to {{ (model.r2_both * 100) | round | int }}%, and each extra £1,000 of income is worth {{ model.per_thousand }} of a tier. Wealth around a ground is not what gets a club up the pyramid. People are.

The people column is the tell. The rich-town clubs draw from tens of thousands; the poor-town clubs from hundreds of thousands, and in the north-west from a million. A big poor town supports a bigger club than a small rich one, and always has.
