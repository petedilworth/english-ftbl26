How far back each level goes

Two different things, and they part company. A season can have a complete league table and no match dates at all — the sixth and seventh tiers come from results grids that carry every score and no date. So anything built on dates covers a shorter range than the tables do, and the two columns say which is which.

A season is flagged incomplete when the matches stored behind it are fewer than a full round-robin — a curtailed season, a part-published file, or a table still being played. Those tables render with a note and are withheld from every records comparison, because a total drawn from a part-played season is not the total that decided anything.
