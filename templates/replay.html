How to read it

  • Each band is a level of the pyramid, the top flight first. Its name is the name it had that season: the First Division becomes the Premier League in 1992/93.
  • Inside a band the clubs are in table order, first place on the left. Where a level is several divisions wide it is ranked across all of them on points, as the Matrix does.
  • A dot that changes band is ringed green on the way up and red on the way down.
  • The lower bands are empty in the early years because the record does not reach them, not because nobody played. What this site knows says where it stops.
