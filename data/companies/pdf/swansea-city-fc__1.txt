Business review
The 2024/25 footbali season was Swansea City’s seventh consecutive season in the EFL Championship It also
was a season which included a significant change in the Club's ownership structure In November 2024, a new
ownership group including Jason Cohen, Andrew Coleman, Brett Cravatt and Nigel Morris acquired the ‘
remaining shares held by former members of the investment group controlled by Jason Levien As a
consequence of this transaction, a significant cash investment was made directly in the Club
Under the direction of Head Coach, Luke Willams, the team initially struggled to achieve consistency, winning
three, drawing three and losing three of our opening nine league fixtures This form continued over the course of
the next few months, before a run of seven defeats in nine games resulted in Luke Willams leaving his role in
February 2025 Luke was replaced by Alan Sheehan for the remaining fixtures of the season, and the team
achieved a positive run of results overall, including a sequence of five consecutive victories in Apnl 2025
Overall, the Club finished the season in a final position of 11th on 61 points (2024 14th place with 57 points) In
recognition of the success achieved by Alan in a relatively short space of time, the Club appointed Alan as the
new permanent Head Coach tn April 2025
|

In March 2025 the Club appointed Tom Gorringe as Chief Executive Officer which was confirmed shortly after
Andrew Coleman informed us of his decision to step down as Chairman at the conclusion of the 2024/25
season Tom has accumulated over 15 years of experence tn professional football, and notably was part of the
leadership team at Brighton & Hove Albion when they were promoted to the Premier League in 2017 Tom also
served as Chief Executive Officer at Bnstol Rovers and achieved promotion to League One with the club in 2022
Andrew remains a board member and co-investor tn the majonty investment group
The Club continued to invest in tts playing squad dunng the penod under review A total of £10,749k of costs
relating to the acquisition of player registrations were capitalised (2024 £5,966k) The Club continued its data
focussed approach to semor player recruitment, particularly in overseas markets, in order to tdentify suitable
acquisitons This was evidenced by the signings of Jksung Eom, Goncato Franco and Florian Bianchini during
Summer 2024, following on from the acquisition of Ronald Pereira Martins from Brazil in the prior Financial Year
Significant gains on disposals of player registrations of £8,128k were recognised during the year (2024
£10,466k), the most significant disposal involving the transfer of Matthew Gnmes to Coventry City in January
2025 Subsequently to the penod end further player registrations were disposed of for a total consideration of
£1,050k (2024 £3,074k)
The Club continued to receive significant financial support from its beneficial owners During the year, the club
secured cash injections totalling £21,029k (2024 £16,547) by way of issues of ordinary shares during the year
Youth Academy
The Club operated a Category 2 status Academy throughout the 2024/25 season and will continue to operate at
this Category for the 2025/26 season Youth Development ts at the forefront of the Club's footballing strategy,

the Senior Team environment, the Academy status will continue to be evaluated to ensure that the Academy is

Principal risks and uncertainties
Relegation
The main risk continues to be relegation from the EFL Championship The Directors are committed to facilitating
the provision of funding necessary for investment in players, coaches and management to ensure the team is
competitive However this funding is dependent on player trading and / or external investment In the event of |
relegation then the Directors wili address this risk by divesting in members of their playing squad to strive to '
achieve an appropriate cost base for the League Division they are competing in The Club has taken steps to '
mitigate the financial impact of relegation by ensuring that so far as possible, player contracts incorporate a |
salary reduction clause in the event of relegation The risk of reduced matchday and commercial revenues ts
mitigated by ensuring that Season Tickets are offered for sale well in advance of the conclusion of the previous |
League season, and that wherever possible commercial contracts are multi-year in duration
Going concern
As set out in greater detail in the accounting polictes in 2 2, the directors acknowledge the difficult financial and
operational conditions the company, along with other Championship football clubs, are expenencing The
directors ensure that the company’s financial position is continually monitored by preparing detailed profit and
loss, balance sheet and cashflow forecasts each financial year considering a range of reasonably foreseeable
scenarios and material uncertainties tn relation to income and costs

This scheme is compulsory for all members of the Professional Footballers Association, unless the
member decides to opt-out of the scheme The company does not make any contributions into the

scheme ts made in these financial statements

participating employers, and where members may have penods of service attributable to several '
participating employers The FLLPLAS comprses both defined benefit and defined contribution sections

final salary section of the scheme was suspended as at 31 August 1999 The company is unable to
identify its share of the assets and the liabilites of the FLLPLAS and therefore accounts for its
