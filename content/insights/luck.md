Every champion is lucky. Of the 124 clubs that won a division in tiers
one to five since 2000/01, 122 took more points than the bookmakers'
odds said their matches were worth – on average almost fourteen more.
Of the 368 that went down, all but thirteen took fewer. That is not a
quirk of the market. A club does not finish top by being exactly as good
as its price; it finishes top by being good and getting the breaks, and
a club that gets relegated was usually bad and unlucky at once.

So luck decides more than anyone admits. Re-rank every finished division
on the points the odds expected, and 109 of 121 end differently: a
different champion, a different club promoted, a different club down.
Leyton Orient went down from League One in 2014/15 with a squad the
market had in seventh. Luton were relegated from the Championship in
2024/25 from what the odds made eleventh. West Bromwich Albion finished
tenth in the Championship in 2021/22 and were second on expected points.

The luckiest season on record is not Leicester's, though Leicester's
2015/16 – 81 points from matches the market priced at 53 – is close.
It is Lincoln City's 2025/26 in League One: 103 points, 73 expected. The
unluckiest is Hyde United's in the Conference in 2013/14, ten points from
matches worth thirty-seven.

And none of it lasts. A club's luck one season tells you nothing about
the next: the correlation is −0.02, as close to zero as a number gets.
Its strength, as the market prices it, carries over at nearly half. Over
twenty-six seasons only five of 126 clubs beat the market by more than
chance would allow, and chance alone would give about six. Plymouth
Argyle come closest to a club that is underrated every year. Nobody has
found the trick.

The shots on target tell a second story, and where they disagree with the
odds is where the page gets interesting. Manchester United won Alex
Ferguson's last title in 2012/13 with 89 points. The odds expected 77.
The shots expected 59 – a mid-table side that kept winning. Coventry in
2003/04 were the reverse: shots worth a promotion push, a finish in
mid-table.
