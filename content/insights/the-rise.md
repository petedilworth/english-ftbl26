Climbing two divisions quickly is not simply relegation played backwards.
Promotion brings a step up in income that arrives before the wage bill
has to rise to match it, and a squad good enough to win one division is
often good enough to compete in the next — which is why momentum is real,
and why back-to-back promotions happen more often than the difficulty of
any single one would suggest.

Holding the new level is the harder part, and it is where these clubs
divide — and not evenly. Of the climbs settled enough to judge, noticeably
more clubs have since slipped back down than have held the division they
reached. The strips below show every season from 1993/94 to today,
