A relegation rarely arrives alone. Some clubs go straight back down the
season after they're promoted; others take two or three divisions in as
many years. This page tracks every club that's moved through the divisions
in a tight cluster since 1993/94 — for better or worse.

Two of the relegation patterns look similar but aren't: a club that's
**held**, then relegated again spent a full season at the lower level
first, unable to arrest the slide. A **sandwiched** relegation is sharper —
promoted straight back up, then sent down again within a year, never
settling at either level.
