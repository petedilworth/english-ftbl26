When a club is wound up, or moved somewhere its supporters will not follow, a
replacement is sometimes founded to carry on in its place. Phoenix clubs
usually start at the bottom of the non-league pyramid with no players, no
ground and no right to their predecessor's record, and climb from there.

They matter because they test what a football club actually is. The Football
League's share, the name and the honours can transfer to a company; the
supporters, the town and the continuity of following can stay behind. Every
phoenix club is an argument that the second list is the club, made by people
who were willing to start again in a park to prove it.
