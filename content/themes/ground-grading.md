Promotion in the non-league pyramid is not settled on the pitch alone. Each
division sets a ground grading standard — covered accommodation, seating,
floodlighting, dressing rooms — and a club that finishes in a promotion place
without meeting the grade for the division above is not promoted. The
deadline usually falls in March, before the season it applies to has ended.

It is the purest form of an off-pitch outcome: a season won on merit and then
not awarded, for reasons of infrastructure the players had no part in. For
clubs without a wealthy owner or a co-operative landlord, the grade can be the
real ceiling on how far the club is allowed to climb.
