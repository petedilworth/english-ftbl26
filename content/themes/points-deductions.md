Not every points deduction comes from insolvency. Clubs have been docked for
fielding an ineligible player, for breaching salary-cost rules, for failing to
fulfil a fixture, and for crowd disorder. The penalties range from a single
point to double figures, and the reasons are often technical rather than
dramatic — an administrative lapse rather than a scandal.

They belong together because a deduction is the one thing in a league table
that didn't happen on the pitch. A club's final position is normally the sum
of its results; for these clubs it isn't, and any reading of their trajectory
that ignores the deduction misreads the season.
