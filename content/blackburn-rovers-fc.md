## Origins

Blackburn Rovers was founded on 5 November 1875 at a meeting at the Leger
Hotel, organised by John Lewis and Arthur Constantine, both old boys of
Shrewsbury School; two former Malvern College pupils were also among the
founding players, giving the club a mixed public-school-old-boys origin
unusual among the mill towns of industrial Lancashire. The club has played
at Ewood Park, on a site used for football since at least 1881, as its
permanent home since 1890.

## Ownership & Finance

Blackburn Rovers has been owned since November 2010 by Venky's London
Limited, the UK holding vehicle of the Rao/Desai family's Venkateshwara
Hatcheries Group, an Indian poultry-to-pharmaceuticals conglomerate based
in Pune. The deal, worth a reported £43 million in total, saw Venky's pay
around £23 million for a 99.9% shareholding — bought from the Walker Trust
— and take on approximately £20 million of existing club debt. No evidence
has emerged of Venky's or the wider VH Group holding stakes in any other
football club; the ownership appears to be Blackburn-specific rather than
part of a multi-club structure.
