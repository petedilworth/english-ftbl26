## Origins

Braintree Town began life in 1898 as Manor Works, a works team of the
Crittall Window Company, taking over the fixtures and most of the players
of a recently defunct earlier club, Braintree F.C., in the North Essex
League. The club was renamed Crittall Athletic in 1921 to align with its
parent company, before becoming Braintree Town by 1983. The nickname "the
Iron" reflects the Crittall Window Company's business — metal-framed
windows, reportedly including frames supplied for RMS Titanic — rather than
heavy industry generally.

## Ownership & Finance

Braintree Town is run as a private limited company without a single
controlling owner; shareholding has been split among a small number of
individuals, with Lee Harding, on the board since 2003, serving as
chairman.

## Infrastructure & Environment

The club has played at Cressing Road, Braintree, since the ground was built
by the Crittall Windows Company in 1923 as a general sports facility,
opening via the fourth annual "Crittall Sports and Show" in front of an
attendance of 6,000.
