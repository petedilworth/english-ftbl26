## Origins

Brackley Town was founded in 1890 in Brackley, Northamptonshire, and was a
prominent local side even in its early years, reportedly playing Tottenham
Hotspur during its formative period.

## Trajectory

The club's one major financial crisis came in February 1999, when it was
forced into voluntary liquidation with debts of more than £100,000. Ray
Styles and Mike Bosher bought the insolvent club's assets and reformed it
under the same name; the reformed club finished bottom of its section and
was relegated that season.

## Ownership & Finance

Ray Styles, co-owner since the 1999 rescue, and Allan Leighton have run the
club as private co-owners rather than through a fan-ownership trust, with
day-to-day leadership under chairman Matt Wise and chief executive Janene
Butters.

## Infrastructure & Environment

The club moved to St James Park, on Churchill Way, in 1974 from its
previous ground on Buckingham Road.
