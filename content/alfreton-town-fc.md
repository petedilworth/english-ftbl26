## Origins

Alfreton Town was formed in 1959 through the merger of two existing
Derbyshire clubs, Alfreton Miners Welfare and Alfreton United, and took its
name from an earlier, unconnected Alfreton Town that had played in the
Midland League during the 1920s. The newly merged club moved into a new
ground on North Street, reportedly provided by the local council, which
has remained its home ever since.

## Trajectory

The club's rise has been built on steady non-league progress rather than
any single dramatic event — promotion through the Northern Counties East
League took it into the Northern Premier League, and a 2010–11 title
brought its first, and so far only, spell in the fifth-tier National
League, ending after four seasons with relegation in 2014–15. The most
unusual episode in the club's history came in 2026, when owner Will Rush
attempted to sell 69% of the club's voting rights as blockchain tokens
through a platform called AlphaFC; the five-day public sale raised only a
fraction of its target and every contribution was refunded. Struggling to
fund the club alone afterwards, Rush put it up for sale that May — the
closest the club has come to a genuine financial crisis, with no
administration, insolvency or points deduction on record at any point in
its history.

## Ownership & Finance

Ownership has sat with a single benefactor rather than a trust or
consortium throughout the club's history: local businessman Wayne Bradley
owned and chaired Alfreton Town for 27 years before handing control to
American businessman Will Rush in June 2025. Rush's brief tenure is best
known for the failed cryptocurrency token sale described above; by May
2026 he had put the club up for sale, with Bradley remaining involved as a
director in an advisory capacity and no new buyer confirmed at time of
writing.

## Infrastructure & Environment

The club has played at North Street since its 1959 formation, on a ground
originally provided by the local council, holding around 3,600 with
roughly 1,500 seated on a grass pitch. The ground's sponsorship name has
changed repeatedly over the years, rebranded "Solana Stadium" for 2025/26
following a fan vote tied to Rush's cryptocurrency venture, after long
being known as the Impact Arena.
