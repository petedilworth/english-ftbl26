## Origins

Boreham Wood F.C. was formed on 24 June 1948 through the amalgamation of
two existing local clubs, Boreham Rovers and Royal Retournez. Boreham
Rovers had itself disbanded in 1939 at the outbreak of the Second World
War; a junior wartime club, Boreham Wood Swifts, carried the town's
footballing presence forward until the 1948 merger created the club in its
current form. The nickname "the Wood" was decided at Boreham Rovers' 1947
AGM, when the club dropped "Rovers," reportedly to establish the name
before another local club could.

## Ownership & Finance

Danny Hunter has chaired the club since the end of the 1999-2000 season and
is its majority shareholder.

## Infrastructure & Environment

The club moved into Meadow Park in 1963 from its previous ground, Eldon
Avenue. In 1999 the original main stand was demolished and replaced with a
500-seat stand, a step tied to meeting grading requirements for
higher-level football.
