## Origins

Bromley F.C. was founded in 1892 and was a founder member of the Southern
League in 1894 and of the London League in 1896. In its first few seasons
the club won the Spartan League, the Isthmian League twice, and the FA
Amateur Cup.

## Trajectory

Bromley did not reach the Football League until promotion via the 2023-24
National League play-off final, entering the EFL for the 2024-25 season for
the first time in the club's 132-year history. Within their debut League
Two campaign, the club began an eight-month redevelopment of Hayes Lane's
East Stand, starting February 2025, investing in ground capacity to meet
higher-tier infrastructure expectations.

## Infrastructure & Environment

Hayes Lane opened in 1938, opened by Stanley Rous, and has hosted Bromley
F.C. ever since; it is also currently used by London City Lionesses. A new
stand opened in 2019, followed by the East Stand redevelopment begun in
2025.
